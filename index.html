Understanding Open Interest (OI) & OI Change
🟢 Put OI / Put Writing (Support): Put sellers take bullish risk. High Put OI marks strong downside support/floor.
🔴 Call OI / Call Writing (Resistance): Call sellers take bearish risk. High Call OI marks upside ceiling/resistance.
📊 Put-Call Ratio (PCR): PCR > 1.0 = Bullish Sentiment, PCR < 0.7 = Bearish Sentiment, PCR > 1.5 = Overbought, PCR < 0.5 = Oversold.
🎯 Max Pain Strike: The strike price at which option buyers lose the most money at expiration (dealer magnet price).
